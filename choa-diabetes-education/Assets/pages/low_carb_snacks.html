Low Carb Snacks

Snack Ideas

Cherry Tomato & String Cheese

½ cup cherry tomatoes + 1 string cheese

Cucumber & Hummus

½ cup cucumber slices + 1 Tbsp hummus

Broccoli & Ranch

½ cup raw broccoli + 1 Tbsp Greek yogurt ranch dressing

Celery & Nut Butter

3 celery sticks + 1 Tbsp nut butter

Graham Cracker & Nut Butter

1 graham cracker square + 1 tsp nut butter

Salami & Cheese Stick

3 pieces of salami + 1 cheese stick

Hard-Boiled Egg & Blueberries

1 hard-boiled egg + ¼ cup blueberries

Tuna Pickle Boats

2 dill pickles with centers scooped out + 1–2 Tbsp tuna salad

Lettuce Wraps

½ cup chicken or tuna salad + 2 lettuce leaves

Bell Pepper Boats

½ cup bell pepper slices + 2 Tbsp goat cheese (spread on top)

Three Ingredient Snack

Cottage Cheese Cucumber Crunch

½ cup cottage cheese + ¼ cup sliced cucumber + 1 Tbsp chopped walnuts

Parmesan Popcorn

1 cup popcorn + 1 Tbsp Parmesan cheese + Italian spices

Roll Ups

2 turkey slices + 2 carrot sticks + 1 tsp honey mustard

Cucumber Sandwiches

6 cucumber slices + 3 small slices deli meat + 3 small slices cheddar cheese

Cucumber Crackers

1 mini or small cheese wedge + 4 cucumber slices + 2 tsp dried cranberries

Bell Pepper Nachos

½ cup bell pepper slices + 2 Tbsp cheddar cheese + 2 Tbsp sliced black olives (microwave to melt cheese)

Zucchini Pizza Bites

5 zucchini slices + 5 tsp tomato sauce + 3 Tbsp mozzarella cheese (bake at 350 degrees until cheese is melted)
