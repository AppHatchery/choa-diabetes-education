Brown Rice

Nutrition

6 servings

Servings

½ cup rice, cooked

Serving Size

Allergies

Gluten free

Ingredients

3 cups

water

Pinch

salt

1.5 cups

brown rice (basmati or jasmine)

Directions

  1. Place water and salt in a pot, cover and bring to a boil.
  2. Once water is boiling, add rice and stir once, keep lid off.
  3. Allow water to come back to boiling.
  4. Once boiling, cover again, reduce heat to simmer.
  5. Allow rice to simmer about 35–45 minutes, until all water has cooked out.
  6. Remove from heat.
  7. Let sit for 5–10 minutes before stirring with a fork to fluff.
